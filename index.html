Pascal's Triangle Expander

Type a binomial raised to a power. Row n of Pascal's triangle gives the coefficients of (a + b)n.

Try: (x + 1)^5(a + b)^6(2x - 3y)^3(x^2 - 1)^4(3 - x)^5

Answer

The power of the first term counts down from n to 0 while the power of the second term counts up from 0 to n. In every row the two powers add to n.
